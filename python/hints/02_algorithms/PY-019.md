## Hint 1
When a new reading arrives, older readings that are **smaller or equal** can never be the maximum of any window that still contains the new one (they leave the window first). So you can forget them immediately.

## Hint 2
Keep only candidate **indices** in a structure that you can pop from both ends: new readings are added at the back (after discarding dominated ones), and an index leaves from the front once it is `k` or more positions behind. What order are the candidate values in, front to back?

## Hint 3
The front is always the current maximum. Start emitting a result once the first full window (index `k - 1`) is reached. Validate `k` before looking at `values`. An alternative that is simpler but slower: a max-heap of `(value, index)` where stale tops (index outside the window) are discarded only when they reach the top.
