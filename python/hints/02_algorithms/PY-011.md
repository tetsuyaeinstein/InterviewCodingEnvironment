## Hint 1
Walk the charges once, left to right. When you stand at position `j`, the policy says "is there any earlier partner for me?" is the only question that matters: the first `j` that has one decides the answer.

## Hint 2
For the charge at `j` you need the amount `target - amounts[j]` somewhere **before** `j`. A dict from amount to position answers that in O(1). Look it up **before** you record the current charge, so a charge never pairs with itself.

## Hint 3
When an amount appears several times, keep the position where you saw it **first** (don't overwrite it). That gives the smallest `i` for the winning `j`. Validate `target_cents` up front: `isinstance(True, int)` is true.
