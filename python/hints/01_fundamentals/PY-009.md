## Hint 1
`str.split()` with no arguments splits on any run of whitespace and drops empty pieces. What does joining the pieces back with `" "` give you?

## Hint 2
Count with a dict or `collections.Counter`, but don't rely on `most_common()` for the final order: equal counts come out in first-seen order.

## Hint 3
Sort the `(name, count)` items with a key that negates the count.
