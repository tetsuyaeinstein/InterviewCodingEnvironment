## Hint 1
Decide whether a transaction counts (status, then guest rule) before you look at its amount. A bad amount on a PENDING row must not break the run.

## Hint 2
Convert every counted amount to `Decimal` without ever passing through `float`. Check `bool` before `int`, because `True` is an `int`.

## Hint 3
Accumulate the unrounded sums per customer, then make one final pass that calls `quantize(Decimal("0.01"), rounding=ROUND_HALF_UP)`. `Decimal("NaN")` parses fine, so check `is_finite()`.
