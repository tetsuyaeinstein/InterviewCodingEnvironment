## Hint 1
Get every timestamp into an aware `datetime` first and reject naive ones explicitly: check `tzinfo is None` (or `utcoffset() is None`) after parsing.

## Hint 2
Convert the instant into the business zone with `astimezone(ZoneInfo(business_tz))` and take `.date()`. Never subtract a fixed number of hours: the offset changes on DST days.

## Hint 3
Accumulate count and sum per date in a dict, then build the result by iterating over the sorted keys.
