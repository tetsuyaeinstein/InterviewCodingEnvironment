# SQL-043 · Late-arriving sales missed by the daily report

**Topic:** CDC, incremental processing & data quality · **Level:** easy · **Interview time:** ~15 min · **Mode:** query

## In plain words

> "Finance published the December sales report with a cut-off time. Which days were understated because sales arrived after the cut-off, and by how much?"

**You return:** one row per December 2025 business date with at least one late sale: `business_date`, `late_txn_count`, `late_amount`, `reported_amount`, `final_amount`, `late_pct`.

**The fine print, in short:**
- Read the cut-off from `pipeline_watermarks` (`daily_sales_report`, `ALL`). Only **completed** sales count. The business date is the UTC date of `transaction_ts`.
- A sale is late only if it arrived at or after the cut-off. Arriving days late but before the cut-off is not late.
- `final_amount` is all completed sales of the date. `late_pct` is late over final, times 100, rounded to 2 decimals.

## Business context

Finance published the December 2025 daily sales report on New Year's night. The report job
included every completed sale that had **arrived in the warehouse before its cut-off**. Some
sales arrived later (offline tills, retried uploads), so the published numbers are too low on some
days. Finance wants to know, per business date, how much was missed and how big the miss is
relative to the corrected total.

## Source tables

| table | grain | columns you need |
|---|---|---|
| `pipeline_watermarks` | one row per (`pipeline_name`, `source_system`) | the report cut-off is the row `pipeline_name = 'daily_sales_report'`, `source_system = 'ALL'` (`watermark_ts`) |
| `transactions` | one row per transaction | `transaction_ts`, `ingested_at`, `transaction_status`, `gross_amount` |

## Requirements

1. Read the cut-off `C` from `pipeline_watermarks` (do not hard-code it).
2. A sale is a transaction with `transaction_status = 'COMPLETED'`.
3. Its business date is the **UTC calendar date of `transaction_ts`**. Only business dates
   **2025-12-01 … 2025-12-31** are in scope.
4. The report contained exactly the sales with `ingested_at < C`. A sale is **late** if
   `ingested_at >= C`.
5. Per business date that has **at least one** late sale, return:
   - `late_txn_count`, `late_amount`: number and sum of `gross_amount` of late sales;
   - `reported_amount`: what the report showed (on-time sales);
   - `final_amount`: the corrected total (all completed sales of that date);
   - `late_pct`: `late_amount / final_amount × 100`, rounded to 2 decimals.

## Output contract

| column | type | notes |
|---|---|---|
| `business_date` | date | one row per affected date |
| `late_txn_count` | integer/bigint | ≥ 1 |
| `late_amount` | numeric | |
| `reported_amount` | numeric | 0 if the whole day was late |
| `final_amount` | numeric | = reported + late |
| `late_pct` | numeric | 2 decimals |

Ordering: none required. Numbers are compared rounded to 2 decimals.

## Illustrative example (not the real data)

Cut-off 2026-01-01 00:00:

| sale | business date | ingested_at | amount | late? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | 12-10 | 12-10 18:00 | 100.00 | no |
| B | 12-10 | 2026-01-03 | 25.00 | yes |
| C | 12-11 | 12-28 | 40.00 | no (late vs its business day, but before the cut-off) |

Output: `12-10 | 1 | 25.00 | 100.00 | 125.00 | 20.00`. 12-11 is not listed.

## Edge cases you must handle

- A sale ingested **exactly at** the cut-off.
- A sale that arrived days late but still before the cut-off (not late for this report).
- A sale late enough to arrive in 2026 but with a December business date.
- Non-completed transactions.

## Constraints

One read-only `SELECT`/`WITH` statement, PostgreSQL 17.

## Clarify before you code (what a strong candidate asks)

- "Late" relative to what: the business day, or the report's cut-off?
- Is the cut-off inclusive or exclusive?
- Business date in UTC or store-local time?
- Should days without late data appear with zeros?
