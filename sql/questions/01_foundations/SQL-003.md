# SQL-003 · Customers inactive in the last 90 days

**Topic:** foundations · **Level:** easy · **Interview time:** ~12 min · **Mode:** query

## Business context

Retention marketing runs a win-back campaign at year end. The audience is every registered
customer who has **not completed a purchase in the 90 days ending on 2025-12-31**. For
personalisation, the campaign also needs each customer's most recent completed purchase date,
if they ever had one.

## Source tables

| table | grain | columns you need |
|---|---|---|
| `customers` | one row per registered customer | `customer_id` |
| `transactions` | one row per checkout transaction | `customer_id`, `transaction_ts` (timestamptz, UTC), `transaction_status` |

## Requirements

1. A purchase is a transaction with `transaction_status = 'COMPLETED'`. Pending, cancelled and
   declined transactions are **not** activity.
2. The anchor date is the fixed date **2025-12-31** (do not use `now()` / `current_date`).
3. The 90-day window is the 90 UTC business dates **2025-10-03 through 2025-12-31, both
   inclusive**, i.e. the timestamp range `[2025-10-03 00:00:00 UTC, 2026-01-01 00:00:00 UTC)`.
4. Return every customer (any customer status) with **no** completed purchase inside that window.
5. `last_completed_purchase_date` is the UTC date of the customer's latest completed purchase
   before the end of the window, or NULL if the customer has never completed a purchase.

## Output contract

| column | type | notes |
|---|---|---|
| `customer_id` | integer | one row per inactive customer |
| `last_completed_purchase_date` | date | UTC date of the latest completed purchase; NULL = never purchased |

Ordering: none required (unordered set).

## Edge cases you must handle

- Customers who never transacted, and customers whose only transactions were cancelled.
- A completed purchase at exactly 00:00:00 on the first day of the window (counts as active).
- A completed purchase one second before the window starts (inactive).
- Recent non-completed activity.

## Constraints

- One read-only `SELECT` / `WITH` statement, valid PostgreSQL 17.
- Watch the window arithmetic: "90 days ending on D" must contain exactly 90 calendar dates.

## Clarify before you code (what a strong candidate asks)

- Is the anchor day itself part of the window? Is the window 90 or 91 dates long?
- Does a pending or cancelled order count as "activity"?
- Should brand-new customers who registered inside the window but never bought be included?
  (Yes: they have no purchase in the window.)
- Which time zone defines the window boundary?
