# SQL-038 · Monthly first-purchase cohorts with month-1 and month-2 repeat rates

**Topic:** dimensional modeling · **Level:** hard · **Interview time:** ~30 min · **Mode:** query

## In plain words

> "For each customer cohort of 2025, based on the month of their first ever completed purchase, how many came back in the next month and in the month after that?"

**You return:** 12 rows, one per cohort month 2025-01 to 2025-12: `cohort_month`, `cohort_size`, `month1_customers`, `month2_customers`, `month1_rate`, `month2_rate`.

**The fine print, in short:**
- Only **completed** purchases by known customers, by UTC month. The cohort is set by the first completed purchase in the whole history. Customers who first bought in 2024 are in no 2025 cohort.
- Month 1 and month 2 are exact months, not cumulative. Several purchases in a month count once.
- Rates are fractions rounded to 2 decimals. If the target month is after December 2025, the rate is NULL, while the count stays a plain number.

## Business context

Growth wants the classic acquisition-cohort table for 2025. A customer's **cohort** is the
calendar month of their **first completed purchase ever** (not their first purchase of the year).
For each 2025 cohort they want the cohort size and how many of those customers bought again in the
**following** calendar month and in the month **after that**, as counts and as rates.

## Source table

| table | grain | columns you need |
|---|---|---|
| `transactions` | one row per checkout transaction | `customer_id` (NULL = guest), `transaction_ts` (timestamptz, UTC), `transaction_status` |

The data covers purchases up to **2025-12-31**. Nothing later is observable.

## Requirements

1. Only `COMPLETED` transactions of known customers count. The month of a purchase is its **UTC
   calendar month**.
2. `cohort_month` = first day of the month of the customer's earliest completed purchase in the
   **whole history** (2024 included). Report cohorts for **2025-01 … 2025-12** only (12 rows). A
   customer who first bought in 2024 belongs to no 2025 cohort.
3. `cohort_size` = customers in the cohort.
4. `month1_customers` = cohort customers with at least one completed purchase in the calendar month
   **immediately after** the cohort month. `month2_customers` = same for the month **two** after
   the cohort month. These are exact months, not cumulative ("within 2 months"); a customer who
   bought only in month 2 is not in month 1.
5. `month1_rate` = `month1_customers / cohort_size`, `month2_rate` likewise, as a fraction rounded
   to 2 decimals (0.35 = 35%).
6. If the target month is after **2025-12** it cannot be observed yet. Then the rate is **NULL**
   (the customer count column still shows the plain count, which is 0).

## Output contract

| column | type | notes |
|---|---|---|
| `cohort_month` | date | first day of the cohort month |
| `cohort_size` | integer | |
| `month1_customers` | integer | |
| `month2_customers` | integer | |
| `month1_rate` | numeric | 2 decimals, NULL if not observable |
| `month2_rate` | numeric | 2 decimals, NULL if not observable |

Ordering: none required.

## Illustrative example (not the real data)

| customer | completed purchase months | cohort | in month 1 | in month 2 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 801 | 2024-12, 2025-01, 2025-02 | 2024-12 (not reported) | | |
| 802 | 2025-03, 2025-04 | 2025-03 | yes | no |
| 803 | 2025-03, 2025-05 | 2025-03 | no | yes |
| 804 | 2025-03 | 2025-03 | no | no |

Cohort 2025-03: size 3, month1 = 1 (0.33), month2 = 1 (0.33).

## Edge cases you must handle

- Customers whose first purchase is in 2024 but who buy again in 2025.
- A pending or cancelled purchase before the first completed one does not define the cohort.
- Several purchases in the same month count once.
- Purchases at the month boundary (e.g. 2025-02-28 23:59:59 then 2025-03-01 00:00:00).
- The last cohorts: months not yet observable are NULL, not 0.00.

## Constraints

- One read-only `SELECT` / `WITH` statement, PostgreSQL 17.
- Production fact is 500M rows: think about the grain you aggregate at.

## Clarify before you code (what a strong candidate asks)

- Is "retained in month 1" exact next month, or any time within a window?
- Which time zone defines the month?
- How should immature cohorts be reported: 0, NULL or hidden?
- Is "first purchase" per customer or per account/household?
