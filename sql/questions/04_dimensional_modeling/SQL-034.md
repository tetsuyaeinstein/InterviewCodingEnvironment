# SQL-034 · Daily account balance snapshot from the posting ledger

**Topic:** dimensional modeling · **Level:** hard · **Interview time:** ~30 min · **Mode:** query

## In plain words

> "Give me a daily balance snapshot for accounts 1 to 5, for 1 to 7 January 2025, with the day's lowest balance too."

**You return:** exactly 35 rows, one per account per day: `account_id`, `balance_date`, `posting_count`, `net_change`, `closing_balance`, `lowest_balance`.

**The fine print, in short:**
- Days are UTC days. A posting exactly at midnight belongs to the day that starts then. Days without postings still get a row (count 0, change 0.00).
- The closing balance includes all history before the window. The lowest balance is the lowest of the start-of-day balance and the balance after each posting that day, in time order, then entry id order.
- An account with no postings has balance 0.00.

## Business context

The stored-value product keeps an append-only **posting ledger**: every deposit, purchase, fee and
adjustment is a signed posting. Treasury and risk do not want to replay the ledger in every
report. They want a **daily balance snapshot** with one row per account per day, including days
without any posting, and including the lowest balance the account reached during the day (risk
uses it to spot accounts that dipped below zero intraday).

Build the snapshot for a pilot customer's accounts for the first week of January 2025.

## Source tables

| table | grain | columns you need |
|---|---|---|
| `accounts` | one row per account | `account_id` |
| `account_ledger` | one row per posting | `entry_id`, `account_id`, `posting_ts` (timestamptz, UTC), `signed_amount` numeric(12,2) (+ credit, − debit) |

## Requirements

1. Scope: accounts **1, 2, 3, 4 and 5**, every UTC calendar day from **2025-01-01 to 2025-01-07
   inclusive**. Output exactly one row per account per day (35 rows), even for accounts or days
   without postings.
2. A posting belongs to the UTC day `[D 00:00:00, D+1 00:00:00)` that contains `posting_ts`. A
   posting at exactly midnight belongs to the day that **starts** at that midnight.
3. `posting_count` / `net_change`: number and sum of the day's postings (0 and 0.00 if none).
4. `closing_balance`: sum of **all** postings of the account with `posting_ts` before the next UTC
   midnight, including any history before 2025-01-01. An account that has never had a posting
   has balance 0.00.
5. `lowest_balance`: the lowest balance during the day, defined as the minimum of
   - the start-of-day balance (all postings before the day's midnight), and
   - the running balance after each of the day's postings, applied in `(posting_ts, entry_id)` order.

   On a day without postings it equals the closing balance. Balances can be negative.
6. Amounts are compared rounded to 2 decimals.

## Output contract

| column | type | notes |
|---|---|---|
| `account_id` | integer | |
| `balance_date` | date | UTC day |
| `posting_count` | integer | 0 if none |
| `net_change` | numeric | 0.00 if none |
| `closing_balance` | numeric | end-of-day balance |
| `lowest_balance` | numeric | intraday low, see rule 5 |

Ordering: none required.

## Illustrative example (not the real data)

Account 801, postings: 03-01 08:00 +50; 03-02 10:00 −70; 03-02 10:00 +30 (same instant, higher
entry_id); 03-04 00:00:00 −5. Window 03-01 … 03-04:

| balance_date | posting_count | net_change | closing_balance | lowest_balance |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 03-01 | 1 | 50.00 | 50.00 | 0.00 |
| 03-02 | 2 | −40.00 | 10.00 | −20.00 |
| 03-03 | 0 | 0.00 | 10.00 | 10.00 |
| 03-04 | 1 | −5.00 | 5.00 | 5.00 |

## Edge cases you must handle

- Several postings with the same `posting_ts` (ledger order is `entry_id`).
- A posting at exactly 00:00:00.
- A posting at 23:59:59 that brings the balance back to exactly 0.
- Days without postings: the balance carries forward.
- An account with no postings at all, and days before an account's first posting.
- `entry_id` order is not always time order.

## Constraints

- One read-only `SELECT` / `WITH` statement, PostgreSQL 17.
- In production the ledger has about 500M postings and 10M accounts. Be ready to explain how the
  snapshot is maintained incrementally.

## Clarify before you code (what a strong candidate asks)

- Which time zone defines the business day, and where does a midnight posting go?
- Should accounts without postings appear (dense snapshot) or be omitted (sparse)?
- Is the balance before the reporting window known (opening balance), or does history start here?
- How are same-timestamp postings ordered?
