# SQL-039 · Monthly active customers by historical region

**Topic:** dimensional modeling · **Level:** medium · **Interview time:** ~20 min · **Mode:** query

## Business context

Regional managers are measured on how many distinct customers bought from "their" region each
month in 2025. The region is the one the customer belonged to **at the time of the purchase**, taken
from the customer type-2 dimension. Customers move: someone who relocated in the middle of a month
shows up in both regions that month, and each regional manager gets credit for them. The
current-state table must not be used, and a customer must never be counted twice in the same region
and month.

## Source tables

| table | grain | columns you need |
|---|---|---|
| `transactions` | one row per checkout transaction | `transaction_id`, `customer_id` (NULL = guest), `transaction_ts` (timestamptz, UTC), `transaction_status` |
| `dim_customer_scd2` | one row per customer version | `customer_id`, `region` (may be NULL = unknown), `effective_from`, `effective_to` (timestamptz) |

Versions are valid on `[effective_from, effective_to)`; the open-ended version has
`effective_to = 9999-12-31 00:00:00+00`.

## Requirements

1. Scope: `COMPLETED` transactions of known customers (`customer_id IS NOT NULL`), with
   `transaction_ts` in **[2025-01-01, 2026-01-01) UTC**. The month is the **UTC calendar month**.
2. The region of a purchase is the `region` of the version containing `transaction_ts` (new
   version wins at the exact change instant).
3. A purchase whose region is NULL (unknown in the dimension) **or** that has no matching version
   is reported in a single group with `region = NULL`. Do not relabel or drop it.
4. `active_customers` = number of **distinct** customers with at least one purchase in that
   month in that region. A customer with several purchases in the same month and region counts
   once. A customer who purchased in two regions in one month counts once in **each**.
5. Only month/region combinations that occur are returned.

## Output contract

| column | type | notes |
|---|---|---|
| `purchase_month` | date | first day of the UTC month |
| `region` | text | historical region; NULL group for unknown |
| `active_customers` | integer | distinct customers |

Ordering: none required.

## Illustrative example (not the real data)

Customer 701 moves from SOUTH to NORTH on 2025-04-10 10:00 and buys on 04-02, 04-05 (SOUTH) and
04-20 (NORTH). Customer 702 (NORTH all year) buys on 04-03 and 04-21.

| purchase_month | region | active_customers |
|---|---|---|
| 2025-04-01 | SOUTH | 1 |
| 2025-04-01 | NORTH | 2 |

## Edge cases you must handle

- A purchase one second before and exactly at a region change.
- A customer who changed region within the month (counted in both).
- Several purchases by one customer in the same month and region.
- Purchases with no matching dimension version or a NULL region.

## Constraints

- One read-only `SELECT` / `WITH` statement, PostgreSQL 17.
- The sum of `active_customers` over regions can legitimately exceed the number of distinct
  customers of the month. Know why.

## Clarify before you code (what a strong candidate asks)

- Should a mover count in both regions, or only in the region of their last (or first) purchase?
- What should an unknown region be called in the report: NULL, `'UNKNOWN'`, or excluded?
- Is the month defined in UTC or head-office time?
- Can this report be summed across months? (Distinct counts are not additive.)
