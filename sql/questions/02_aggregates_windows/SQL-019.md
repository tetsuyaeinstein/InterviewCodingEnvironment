# SQL-019 · Product share of store revenue without double counting

**Topic:** aggregates & windows · **Level:** medium · **Interview time:** ~15 min · **Mode:** query

## In plain words

> "At store 5 in November 2025, how much did each product earn, and what share of the store revenue is that?"

**You return:** one row per product sold: `store_id` (always 5), `product_id`, `net_revenue`, `store_net_revenue`, `pct_of_store_revenue`.

**The fine print, in short:**
- Only lines of **completed** transactions at store 5, UTC dates in November 2025.
- Net is quantity times unit price minus the line discount. Several lines of one product make one row.
- The store total is the same on every row. The percentage is rounded to 2 decimals.

## Business context

The manager of store **5** wants to know how November 2025 revenue splits across products: for
each product, its revenue and its **percentage of the store's total**. Finance will check that
the product revenues add up to the store total and the percentages to 100. An earlier report
joined baskets to lines and to other tables carelessly, so some baskets were counted several
times.

## Source tables

| table | grain | columns you need |
|---|---|---|
| `transactions` | one row per checkout transaction | `transaction_id`, `store_id`, `transaction_ts` (timestamptz, UTC), `transaction_status` |
| `transaction_items` | one row per order line | `transaction_id`, `product_id`, `quantity`, `unit_price`, `discount_amount` |

## Requirements

1. Scope: lines of `COMPLETED` transactions at `store_id = 5` with `transaction_ts` in
   `[2025-11-01 00:00:00 UTC, 2025-12-01 00:00:00 UTC)`.
2. Line net revenue = `quantity × unit_price − discount_amount` (the discount covers the whole line).
3. One row per product sold in scope; several lines of the same product (even inside one
   transaction) are **one** row.
4. `store_net_revenue`: total net revenue of the store in scope (same value on every row).
5. `pct_of_store_revenue = 100 × net_revenue / store_net_revenue`, rounded to 2 decimals.

## Output contract

| column | type | notes |
|---|---|---|
| `store_id` | integer | always 5 |
| `product_id` | integer | |
| `net_revenue` | numeric | 2 decimals |
| `store_net_revenue` | numeric | 2 decimals |
| `pct_of_store_revenue` | numeric | 2 decimals |

Ordering: none required.

## Illustrative example (not the real data)

Store 900 has lines: product 1 → 30.00; product 2 → 10.00 + 10.00 (two lines); product 3 →
50.00 (but its basket was cancelled).

| product_id | net_revenue | store_net_revenue | pct |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 30.00 | 50.00 | 60.00 |
| 2 | 20.00 | 50.00 | 40.00 |

## Edge cases you must handle

- A cancelled basket with a large amount.
- The same product on two lines of one transaction.
- Line discounts.
- Transactions outside the month (the neighbouring month at the same store).
- Multi-line baskets: the header amount must not be repeated per line.

## Constraints

- One read-only `SELECT` / `WITH` statement, valid PostgreSQL 17.

## Clarify before you code (what a strong candidate asks)

- Is the total before or after discounts, and does it include refunds?
- Is the percentage of the store total or of the sum of the rows shown?
- How should rounding differences (percentages not summing to exactly 100) be handled?
