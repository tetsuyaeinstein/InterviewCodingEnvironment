# SQL-014 · Running daily spend per customer

**Topic:** aggregates & windows · **Level:** easy · **Interview time:** ~12 min · **Mode:** query

## Business context

A loyalty campaign ran in September and October 2025 for a group of small-business and test
accounts (customer IDs **15 through 26**). Marketing wants a "spend journey" per customer: for
each day the customer bought something, how much they spent that day and how much they had
spent **in the campaign so far**.

## Source table

| table | grain | columns you need |
|---|---|---|
| `transactions` | one row per checkout transaction | `customer_id`, `transaction_ts` (timestamptz, UTC), `gross_amount`, `transaction_status` |

## Requirements

1. Scope: customers `15 … 26` (inclusive), `COMPLETED` transactions with a UTC business date
   from 2025-09-01 to 2025-10-31 inclusive, i.e. `transaction_ts` in
   `[2025-09-01 00:00:00 UTC, 2025-11-01 00:00:00 UTC)`.
2. Spend = `gross_amount`. Refunds are **not** deducted in this report.
3. One row per customer per **purchase day** (UTC calendar date with at least one completed
   transaction). Several transactions on the same day become one row.
4. `daily_spend`: the customer's spend on that day.
5. `running_total`: the customer's spend from 2025-09-01 up to and including that day. It starts
   from zero for every customer; nothing before 2025-09-01 counts.
6. Customers in the range with no completed purchase in the period are not returned.

## Output contract

| column | type | notes |
|---|---|---|
| `customer_id` | integer | |
| `purchase_date` | date | UTC calendar date |
| `daily_spend` | numeric | 2 decimals |
| `running_total` | numeric | 2 decimals |

Ordering: none required.

## Illustrative example (not the real data)

| customer | transactions | rows returned (date, daily, running) |
|---|---|---|
| 701 | Sep 3 40.00; Sep 3 15.00; Sep 9 20.00 | (Sep 3, 55.00, 55.00), (Sep 9, 20.00, 75.00) |
| 702 | Sep 4 30.00 CANCELLED; Sep 5 10.00 | (Sep 5, 10.00, 10.00) |

## Edge cases you must handle

- Several completed transactions on one day, including two at exactly the same timestamp.
- A day with a cancelled transaction next to a completed one.
- A later non-completed transaction.
- Customers in the ID range who did not buy in the period.

## Constraints

- One read-only `SELECT` / `WITH` statement, valid PostgreSQL 17.

## Clarify before you code (what a strong candidate asks)

- Is the running total per customer, or across all customers?
- Does the running total start at the beginning of the period or at the customer's first ever
  purchase?
- One row per transaction or per day?
- Should refunds reduce the spend?
