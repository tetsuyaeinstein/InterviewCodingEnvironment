## Hint 1
Don't filter to the late rows in `WHERE`: you need the on-time rows of the same day to compute the final total.

## Hint 2
Compute a boolean "is late" per sale (`ingested_at` compared to the cut-off), group by business date, and aggregate conditionally (`FILTER (WHERE ...)` or `SUM(CASE ...)`).

## Hint 3
Keep only the dates that have late sales with `HAVING`. Check the boundary: a sale ingested at exactly the cut-off was not in a report built with `ingested_at < cut-off`.
