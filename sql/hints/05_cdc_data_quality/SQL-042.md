## Hint 1
Which timestamp decides whether the previous run could have seen a row: when the sale happened, or when the row landed? Filter on that one only.

## Hint 2
Turn the watermark row into the run's bounds first (a small CTE with `from` and `to`), then join or cross join it to the transactions.

## Hint 3
Half-open means `>= lower AND < upper`. `BETWEEN` is inclusive on both ends, which double-processes boundary rows on the next run. The overlap flag is simply `ingested_at < watermark`.
