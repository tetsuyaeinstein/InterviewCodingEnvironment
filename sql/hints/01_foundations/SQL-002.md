## Hint 1
Write the date range as a half-open interval on the raw `transaction_ts` column: `>=` the first instant, `<` the first instant after the range. What is the first instant after 2025-03-16?

## Hint 2
`BETWEEN '2025-03-01' AND '2025-03-16'` on a timestamp means "up to 2025-03-16 00:00:00", which silently drops almost the whole last day. Wrapping the column in a function to compare dates works but stops an index on the column from being used.

## Hint 3
Group by the store and the UTC date expression `(transaction_ts AT TIME ZONE 'UTC')::date`. Only completed rows should reach the aggregation, so no zero-count groups can appear.
