## Hint 1
Draw the relationships: one transaction has N lines and M refunds. If you join the header to both children at once, how many rows does one transaction with 2 lines and 2 refunds produce?

## Hint 2
Bring each child to the grain of the transaction **before** it meets the other child: one row per transaction with its total quantity, one row per transaction with its total refunded amount. Then the joins are 1:1.

## Hint 3
Filter the transactions by sale date only. The refunds you want are "refunds of those transactions", so do not filter `refund_ts`. Use an outer join for refunds (most transactions have none) and COALESCE the missing total to 0.00.
