## Hint 1
The output grain is one row per customer, and some customers have no matching transactions at all. Which table must drive the query so that nobody disappears?

## Hint 2
If you filter the transactions side of an outer join in the `WHERE` clause, rows of customers without a match are filtered away again. Put the status condition where it does not do that, or aggregate the completed transactions first and join the result.

## Hint 3
After an outer join, `COUNT(*)` counts the customer row itself (1, not 0) and `SUM` of nothing is NULL. Count a column from the transaction side, and turn the missing sum into 0.00.
