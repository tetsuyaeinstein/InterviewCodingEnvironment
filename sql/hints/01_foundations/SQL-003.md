## Hint 1
Count the dates: a window of 90 dates ending on 2025-12-31 starts on 2025-10-03. `date '2025-12-31' - 90` is 2025-10-02, which would make the window 91 dates long.

## Hint 2
The audience is defined by the **absence** of something. Start from all customers and keep those for whom no completed purchase exists in the window. Customers with no transactions at all must survive this step.

## Hint 3
The last purchase date is a separate question from "active in the window": compute the latest completed `transaction_ts` per customer (NULL if none), and decide activity with a separate condition, for example a filtered count or an existence test.
