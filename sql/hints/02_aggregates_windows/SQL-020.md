## Hint 1
The balance after an entry is the sum of that account's entries up to and including it, in a well-defined order. Think about which ordering columns make the order total, and about what "up to this entry" means when two entries share a timestamp.

## Hint 2
When a window has an `ORDER BY` and no explicit frame, rows that tie on the ordering values are treated as one group and see each other. Check that behaviour on an account with two postings at the same timestamp, and make the frame explicit at row level.

## Hint 3
`SUM(signed_amount) OVER (PARTITION BY account_id ORDER BY posting_ts, entry_id ROWS BETWEEN UNBOUNDED PRECEDING AND CURRENT ROW)`. For the "enters" flag you need the balance of the previous entry in the same order: `LAG(balance, 1, 0)` over the same ordering, computed one level up.
