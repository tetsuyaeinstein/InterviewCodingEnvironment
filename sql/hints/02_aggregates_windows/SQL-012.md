## Hint 1

## Hint 2

## Hint 3
Either keep rank-2 values with a rank that does not skip numbers and aggregate per store with a conditional `MAX(...) FILTER (...)`, or compute `MAX(amount)` among the rows whose amount is strictly below the store's maximum, with an outer join back to the store list.
