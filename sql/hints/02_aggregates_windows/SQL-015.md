## Hint 1
Months without sales have no rows in `transactions`. Build the list of (store, month) pairs yourself, and attach monthly revenue to it with zero as the default.

## Hint 2
"Previous row" equals "previous calendar month" only if the series has no gaps and starts early enough. Make sure the month before the first report month exists in the series, and drop it at the very end.

## Hint 3
`LAG(revenue) OVER (PARTITION BY store_id ORDER BY month_start)` on a dense series that starts in January, then guard the division with `NULLIF(prev, 0)` and round. Filter to February … December in the outer query.
