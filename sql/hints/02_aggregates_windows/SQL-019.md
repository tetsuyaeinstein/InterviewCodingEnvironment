## Hint 1
Which table holds the amount you need at the grain of a product? Do not use a basket-level amount once you have joined to lines.

## Hint 2
First reduce to one row per product with its revenue. The store total is then just a total over those rows, so you do not need a second pass over the base tables.

## Hint 3
`SUM(net_revenue) OVER ()` on the per-product rows gives the store total next to each row; divide, multiply by 100, round.
