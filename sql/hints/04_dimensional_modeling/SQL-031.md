## Hint 1
The customer key alone is not enough to find the right dimension row: a customer has several
rows. Which extra condition on the timestamp picks exactly one of them?

## Hint 2
"Contains" on a half-open interval means `start <= t` and `t < end`. Put that condition in the
**join condition** of an outer join, not in the WHERE clause, so unmatched facts survive.

## Hint 3
Filter the fact (status and date range) first, outer-join the dimension on customer plus the
interval condition, then outer-join `customers` for today's region. Count rows afterwards: it must
equal the number of in-scope transactions.
