## Hint 1
Work at customer × month grain. First find each customer's cohort month over their whole history,
and only then restrict to the 2025 cohorts.

## Hint 2
For each cohort customer, ask whether a customer-month row exists exactly one month, and exactly
two months, after the cohort month. Counting rows from a LEFT JOIN, or conditional counts over
a set that carries the cohort month on each row, both answer it.

## Hint 3
Compare month arithmetic on dates truncated to the first of the month. Rates need numeric division
and rounding. A target month later than December 2025 has not happened in the data yet, so decide
what the rate should be there.
