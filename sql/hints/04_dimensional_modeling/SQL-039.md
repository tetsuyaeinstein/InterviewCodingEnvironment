## Hint 1
First attach the historical region to every purchase with a point-in-time join, keeping purchases
that find no version (outer join, with the time condition inside the join condition).

## Hint 2
Think about what a "row" in your intermediate set means. The metric counts customers per month per
region, so the grain you want to aggregate is (month, region, customer) before counting.

## Hint 3
Either de-duplicate to that grain and then count rows, or count distinct customer ids per group.
Grouping by a NULLable column puts all NULLs in one group, which is exactly the unknown-region bucket.
