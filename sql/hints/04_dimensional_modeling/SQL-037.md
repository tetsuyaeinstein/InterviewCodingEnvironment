## Hint 1
Decide the grain before you pair anything: you want one row per (basket, product). What do you do
about a product on two lines?

## Hint 2
Joining that set to itself on the basket produces every ordered pair, including a product with
itself. One inequality on the product ids removes both the self-pairs and the mirrored duplicates.

## Hint 3
Group by the two ids and count baskets; the threshold is a HAVING. If you prefer to avoid a
self-join of the lines, think of each basket as a sorted list of distinct products and generate the
index pairs inside it.
