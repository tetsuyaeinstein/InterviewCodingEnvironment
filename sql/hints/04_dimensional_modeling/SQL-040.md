## Hint 1
Each measure has its own natural grain and its own date: sales and units are recognised at the
transaction time, refunds at the refund time. Lines and refunds are both one-to-many from the
transaction.

## Hint 2
If you join header, lines and refunds first and then sum, every line is repeated once per refund and
every refund once per line. Aggregate each measure on its own to one row per month before anything
meets anything else (or stack the streams instead of joining them).

## Hint 3
Start from a generated list of the four months so the last month survives, combine the per-month
results with outer joins (or sum over a stacked set), and COALESCE missing measures to 0 before
computing the net.
