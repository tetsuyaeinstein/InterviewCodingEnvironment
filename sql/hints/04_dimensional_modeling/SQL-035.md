## Hint 1
Do the time-zone conversion first, on the instant, and only then take the date. `AT TIME ZONE`
with an IANA zone name applies the correct offset for each instant, including DST.

## Hint 2
PostgreSQL's date formatting has separate patterns for the ISO year (`IYYY`) and the calendar
year (`YYYY`). `IW` is the ISO week number. Mixing `YYYY` with `IW` produces the "2024-W01" bug.
The Monday of the week is what `date_trunc` returns for the `'week'` field.

## Hint 3
Filter on the local date: the Monday of 2025-W01 is 2024-12-30 and the Monday of 2026-W01 is
2025-12-29. Then group by label and Monday, with a distinct count of customers and a plain count of
transactions.
