## Hint 1
You cannot find dates that have no rows by looking at the rows. First produce every date of the quarter as its own set of rows.

## Hint 2
PostgreSQL has a set-returning function that produces a series of numbers (or timestamps). The number of days between two dates is plain date subtraction, and `date + integer` is a date.

## Hint 3
Compare the calendar with the set of dates that have a completed sale: either `LEFT JOIN` and keep rows where the right side is NULL, or `NOT EXISTS`. Compute the sale date from the timestamp in UTC and apply the status filter before the comparison.
