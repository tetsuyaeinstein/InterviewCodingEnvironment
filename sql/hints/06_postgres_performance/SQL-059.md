## Hint 1
Partition pruning compares the query's predicate on the **partition key** with each partition's bounds. Look at the plan of the slow version: what does each partition's `Filter` look like?

## Hint 2
The planner can only reason about `sale_date` compared with a constant. Anything that computes something *from* `sale_date` first (a text, a truncated value, an extracted part) is opaque to it.

## Hint 3
Express the month as two comparisons on the bare column: `>=` the first day, `<` the first day of the next month. Then check the plan: the other 24 partitions, including the default partition, must be absent.
