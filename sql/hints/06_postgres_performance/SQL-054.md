## Hint 1
Do the two kinds of key separately (one aggregation each), then stack the results. `GROUP BY customer_id` keeps NULL as its own group: count it, then give it a printable label.

## Hint 2
Rank within each key kind with a window function partitioned by the kind. Remember the tie-break rule: ties are common among the ordinary customers, and the ranks must be unique.

## Hint 3
The percentage denominator is the total number of transactions for both kinds. For the rationale: compare the top share to the "fair share" of one distribution (1/60, about 1.7%), and think about what a single hot key does to the slowest of 60 workers.
