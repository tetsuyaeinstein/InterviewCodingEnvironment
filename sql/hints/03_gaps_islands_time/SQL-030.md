## Hint 1
Missing days have no rows in the event table, so you cannot find them by reading events alone. You need the set of days that *should* have a report.

## Hint 2
Generate the calendar for the period, join it to devices on the in-service rule, and remove the (device, day) pairs that have a SCHEDULED event whose `event_ts` falls on that UTC day. What remains are missing days.

## Hint 3
Consecutive missing days of a device form a gap: `day - row_number()` per device gives a gap key; group to get start, end and count. Alternatively, skip the calendar: take distinct report days per device, add sentinel days just outside the device's service window, and use `LEAD` to find jumps of more than one day.
