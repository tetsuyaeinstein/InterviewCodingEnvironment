## Hint 1
The threshold is about a day's total, not about individual purchases. What grain must the data be at before you compare against 100.00?

## Hint 2
Build customer x UTC day totals from completed transactions only, keep the days with total > 100.00, and only then look for consecutive runs among those days.

## Hint 3
Once you have the qualifying days, the run logic is the same as a purchase-day streak: `day - row_number()` per customer gives a run key; group, keep `COUNT(*) >= 5`, and pick the run with the smallest start per customer (e.g. `DISTINCT ON` or a row number).
