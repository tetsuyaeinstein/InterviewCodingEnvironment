## Hint 1
Two steps: identify the cohort (first completed purchase over all history, then filter to February), then look at what each cohort member did in the next 7 calendar days.

## Hint 2
Work with UTC dates, not timestamps: `first_purchase_date + 7` is a date, and comparing dates sidesteps hours and minutes entirely.

## Hint 3
LEFT JOIN the cohort to the customer's distinct purchase days in (day 0, day 0 + 7], then aggregate per customer with boolean aggregates. Customers with no matching rows produce NULL aggregates; turn those into false.
